Rules

A few rules. Nothing crazy.

The point of the rules is to keep the server a place people actually want to be. Keep it simple, keep it respectful, and you'll never have a problem here.

01

Be respectful

No harassment, hate speech, or toxic behavior. Treat other players the way you'd want to be treated. Staff decisions are final.

02

No griefing

Don't destroy or steal from other players' builds without permission. Accidents happen — report them. Intentional griefing gets you banned.

03

No cheating or hacks

X-ray, fly hacks, kill aura — none of it. Play fair. If you're not sure whether something is allowed, ask a staff member first.

04

No spam or advertising

Don't spam chat or advertise other servers in public channels. Keep the Discord and in-game chat clean and relevant.

05

Listen to staff

Staff have the final say. If a staff member asks you to do something, do it. Disputes go through the appropriate channels, not in public chat.

06

No pay-to-win

Everything in the game is earnable through gameplay. No ranks to buy, no paywalls. If something looks like it costs money, it doesn't.
