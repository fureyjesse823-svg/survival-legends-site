The Server

Built for survival, designed for community

Survival Legends is a vanilla survival SMP where the focus is on playing together, building something worth remembering, and earning your place in the world. No anarchy, no griefing-for-fun — just good survival Minecraft with people who want to be here.

🏔️

Vanilla Survival

The real deal — explore, gather, craft, and build in a world that feels like Minecraft should. No gimmicks, no pay-to-win, just survival.

Core Experience

Your base is yours. Claim land, protect what you've built, and play without looking over your shoulder. Peace of mind so you can focus on creating.
